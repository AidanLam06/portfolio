Design Philosophy & Process

This portfolio serves as an archive for the user interfaces I will design throughout the SEG3125 (Analysis and Design of User Interfaces) course at the University of Ottawa. My objective this semester is to explore the different spaces of UI/UX design, including video games, business services, e-commerce platforms and data analytics

I rely on empirical frameworks such as the Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g) Articles regarding visual hierarchy, error prevention, and user control, alongside standard platform-specific design patterns.
